A visual essay

Every Score Is a Crowd

27,075 games. One number each. We went looking for good games and kept finding the people who rated them.

Act 1 — The fog

A bigger crowd does not hand out better scores

Every dot is a game: how many people reviewed it, and how many of them were positive. Click a band — the five columns behind the bars — and watch which end of the cloud moves.

Act 2 — The rulers

Every kind of game is judged against its own crowd

Nineteen kinds of games, each scored net of the crowd that rates it. Hover a label to read its interval; click it to open the explorer below.

Act 3 — The cut

The same chart, cut three ways

One line, three cuts. The slope that looks obvious disappears as soon as the chart is allowed to see who is being compared.

Act 4 — Your turn

Pick a kind of game and a crowd, and see who is left

Every cell shows the middle 80% of scores for that kind of game at that audience size. Cells with fewer than 15 games are shown as empty.

Data: Steam Store Games (Kaggle, nikdavis), a SteamSpy snapshot from September 2019. SteamSpy is an unofficial project and is not associated with Valve.

Built with D3 v7. Every number on this page is precomputed; nothing is aggregated in the browser.
